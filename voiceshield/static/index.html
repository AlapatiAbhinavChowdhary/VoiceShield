    How to read this

    Score is the detector's log-odds that a 4 s window is AI-generated: below 0 leans real, above 0 leans fake.

    Window probability compares that score with this line's calibrated cut-off: above 50% the window counts as suspicious.

    Risk averages recent windows and adds the voiceprint and scam-language checks. VERIFY from 45, HOLD from 75 with 3 of 5 windows flagged.

    Audio type is what the Step 8c back-end recognises: clean, phone line, noise suppression, Bluetooth or Opus.
